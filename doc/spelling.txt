              Spell checking helpers for Vim editor          by @diacus

Mappings to turn the spell checking on and off quickly, and to correct
the words flagged as misspelled.  This plugin defines no commands and no
global variables.

NORMAL MODE MAPPINGS                                  *spelling-mappings*

<F2>        Toggles spell checking.

            When the check is turned ON, the 'cursorline' is disabled,
            the syntax highlighting is turned off, so the squiggly marks
            of the misspelled words are not affected by colors, and
            <Tab> is mapped in both, normal and insert mode, to show
            the completion menu with the suggestions for the word under
            or before the cursor.

            When the check is turned OFF, everything is restored.

            Note: if the buffer is 'readonly', regardless of the current
            state, spelling is turned off and 'cursorline' is enabled.

<Leader><Space>
            Shows the spelling suggestions for the word before the
            cursor.  Internally, this mapping moves one character to the
            right, and triggers insert-mode completion with <C-X><C-S>,
            see |ins-completion-menu|.
